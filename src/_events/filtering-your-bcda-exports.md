As a bulk API, BCDA exports tens of thousands of records for every attributed person on Medicare. In practice, BCDA users look for specific types of Medicare patient claims data to support their tracking, care coordination, reporting, and other goals. With filtering parameters, including the new `_typeFilter` (v3), you can limit the scope of your exports by a variety of factors from content- to time-based. This office hours starts with a practical overview of parameters. The informal presentation will be followed by open Q&A on any area of interest, from filtering to claims data in general.
